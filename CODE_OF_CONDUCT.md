# Code of Conduct

OpenChessLab should be a welcoming technical space for people of every background and experience level. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related public communication.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people's character or identity.
- Assume good intent while taking the impact of your words seriously.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent, and differences in communication style or chess experience.
- Keep discussion relevant and help newcomers understand decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, targeted insults, repeated disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reports are not acceptable. Publishing private correspondence or identifying information without permission is prohibited.

## Reporting and Enforcement

The repository owner must provide a private conduct-reporting contact in the repository About section before inviting public community participation. No monitored address or enforcement team is claimed before publication. Do not put sensitive reports in a public issue.

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request corrections, restrict participation, or temporarily or permanently ban people from project spaces. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made with the privacy and safety of affected people in mind. A person involved in a report should not decide its outcome alone.

An appeal may be submitted through the same private contact, requesting review by an uninvolved maintainer where one is available. No response-time guarantee or staffed moderation service is asserted by this repository.
